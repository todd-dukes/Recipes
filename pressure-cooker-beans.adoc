= Pressure Cooker Bean Guide (Emeril Lagasse)
:sectnums:

== General Instructions for Cooking Beans

To ensure perfect texture and safety when cooking *unsoaked* beans in your Emeril Lagasse pressure cooker, follow these core steps:

. *Rinse and Sort:* Always rinse dry beans thoroughly in a colander and pick out any debris or shriveled beans.
. *Liquid Ratio:* Use a minimum ratio of *3 to 4 cups of water* for every *1/2 cup of dry beans*. The pot must never be
  filled more than halfway when cooking beans, as they expand and foam.
. *No Acid Early:* Do not add acidic ingredients (tomatoes, vinegar, lemon juice) until *after* the beans are fully
  tender, as acid prevents softening.
. *Natural Release:* For all beans, allow the pressure to release *naturally for at least 15-20 minutes* after the timer
  ends. Quick-releasing can cause skins to burst while leaving the interior crunchy.
. *Safety Note:* Red Kidney Beans contain natural toxins (lectins) that are destroyed by high heat. The pressure cooking
  times below are sufficient to eliminate these toxins, but ensure the cooker reaches full pressure.

== Unsoaked Bean Cooking Chart (Fresh Beans)

*Target Yield:* Equivalent to 1/2 cup dry Red Kidney Beans (approx. 1.5 cups cooked).

[cols="1,1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Bean Type
| Dry Amount Needed
| Water Amount (Min)
| Cooking Time (High Pressure)

| *Red Kidney Beans*
| 1/2 cup
| 3-4 cups
| 20-25 mins

| *Lentils (Brown/Green)*
| 2/3 cup
| 3 cups
| 8-10 mins

| *Navy Beans*
| 1/2 cup
| 3-4 cups
| 25-30 mins

| *Pinto Beans*
| 1/2 cup
| 3-4 cups
| 15-20 mins

| *Garbanzo (Chickpeas)*
| 1/2 cup
| 3-4 cups
| 35-40 mins

| *Northern Beans*
| 1/2 cup
| 3-4 cups
| 25-30 mins
|===

*Note:* Always allow for a *Natural Release* of 15-20 minutes after the cooking time completes. Do not use Quick
 Release. These times assume fresh beans (packed within the last year).

== Seasoning Suggestions

* t - teaspoon
* T - tablespoon

=== Lima beans to be paired with steak

* 1/2 t paprika
* 1/2 t garlic powder
* 1/2 t onion powder
* 1/2 t black pepper
* 1/2 t salt
* 1/2 t thyme
* 1     bay leaf (remove before serving)

=== Red Kidney beans paired with BBQ chicken

* 1/2 t ground cumin
* 1/2 t smoked paprika
* 1/2 t garlic powder
* 1/2 t onion powder
* 1 t   worcestershire
* 1 T   brown sugar
* 1/2 t salt
* 1     bay leaf (remove before serving)
