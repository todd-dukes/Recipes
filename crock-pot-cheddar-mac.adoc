= Cheddar Mac

== Ingredients

* 1 (16 oz) package macaroni
* 2 cups half-and-half, milk, or heavy cream
* 1 can cheddar cheese soup
* 1/2 cup butter, melted
* 4 cups shredded cheddar cheese

== Directions

. Cook pasta according to package directions and drain.
. Spray crock pot with cooking oil.
. Combine pasta with remaining ingredients and place in crock pot.
. Cover and cook on LOW for 2 to 2 1/2 hours.
