
= Roasted Tomato & Sausage Pasta Sauce
:toc:
:icons: font
:source-highlighter: coderay
:imagesdir: images
:experimental:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Prep Time | 20 minutes
| Cook Time | 60–75 minutes
| Total Time | ~1¾ hours
| Servings    | 6 generous portions (perfect for 1 lb / 454 g dried pasta)
|===

NOTE: This is a deeply flavorful roasted vegetable tomato sauce with browned Italian sausage folded in. Roasting + blending gives you an incredibly smooth, sweet, and smoky sauce without any canned tomatoes or long simmering.

== Ingredients

=== Vegetables for roasting
|===
| Amount | Ingredient

| 4 lb (1.8 kg) | fresh ripe tomatoes (Roma, plum, or on-the-vine), cored and halved
| 8 oz (225 g) | yellow or red onion, cut into large chunks (≈ 1 very large or 2 medium)
| 4–5 oz (110–140 g) | carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks (1–2 medium)
| 4–5 oz (110–140 g) | celery, cut into 1-inch chunks (2–3 stalks)
| 6–8 cloves | garlic, peeled (left whole)
| 3–4 Tbsp | extra-virgin olive oil
| 2 tsp | kosher salt
| to taste | freshly ground black pepper
| 3–4 sprigs | fresh oregano (or 1 tsp dried)
|===

=== After roasting
|===
| Amount | Ingredient

| 6–8 large leaves | fresh basil
| 1–1½ tsp | sugar (optional, only if tomatoes need sweetness)
| 1–2 Tbsp | tomato paste (optional, for deeper color/flavor)
| pinch | red pepper flakes (optional)
|===

=== Sausage & Pasta
|===
| Amount | Ingredient

| 1 lb (450 g) | Italian sausage links (sweet or hot), casings removed, chopped into ½-inch pieces
| 1 lb (454 g) | dried pasta (spaghetti, rigatoni, penne, etc.)
| as needed | reserved pasta cooking water
| for serving | grated Parmigiano-Reggiano or Pecorino Romano
| for serving | extra fresh basil and good olive oil
|===

== Instructions

. Preheat oven to 425 °F (220 °C). Prepare two large rimmed sheet pans (important: do not crowd or the veggies will steam instead of caramelize).

. Place halved tomatoes cut-side up on the sheet pans. Scatter onion, carrot, celery, and garlic around them.

. Drizzle everything with 3–4 Tbsp olive oil, sprinkle with 2 tsp kosher salt and plenty of black pepper. Tuck oregano sprigs among the vegetables.

. Roast 45–60 minutes, rotating pans halfway, until tomatoes are collapsed and everything has nice charred edges.

. Remove from oven, discard woody oregano stems, and let cool 10–15 minutes.

. Transfer roasted vegetables + all pan juices to a blender in batches. Add fresh basil leaves. Blend until completely smooth (or leave slightly chunky if you prefer). Thin with a little water only if absolutely necessary.

. While vegetables roast (or while blending), brown the chopped sausage in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat until deeply golden and cooked through. Drain excess fat if desired.

. Pour the blended sauce into the pot with the sausage. Add tomato paste and red pepper flakes if using. Simmer gently 10–15 minutes. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, and sugar.

. Cook 1 lb pasta in heavily salted water until just al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.

. Drain pasta and add directly to the sauce. Toss over low heat, adding reserved pasta water a splash at a time until every strand is perfectly coated.

. Serve immediately with plenty of grated cheese, torn basil, and a drizzle of olive oil.

== Notes & Tips

- The sauce freezes beautifully for up to 3 months.
- For an even richer sauce, deglaze the sausage pan with a splash of red wine before adding the blended vegetables.
- If your tomatoes are out of season, add the optional 1–2 Tbsp tomato paste — it boosts color and umami.

Enjoy your smoky, sweet, restaurant-worthy roasted tomato sausage sauce! 🍝
