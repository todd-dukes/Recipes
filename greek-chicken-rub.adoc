= Greek Chicken Rub (Half Batch)

Should be enough for 2 chicken breasts or 3-4 thighs.

== Ingredients
* 1–1.5 Tbsp dried Oregano
* 0.5–1 Tbsp Granulated Garlic
* 0.5 Tbsp Paprika
* 0.5–0.75 tsp Onion Powder
* 0.5 tsp dried Thyme
* 0.25–0.5 tsp dried Rosemary (crush lightly)
* 0.5–0.75 tsp Pink Salt (or to taste)
* 0.25–0.5 tsp Black Pepper (freshly ground)

== Optional
* Tiny pinch of Ground Cinnamon
* Small pinch of Cayenne or Chili Powder

== Instructions
1. Mix all spices together in a small bowl.
2. Pat chicken pieces dry.
3. Lightly oil chicken, then press rub mixture evenly onto both sides.
4. Let sit 15–30 minutes (or refrigerate up to a few hours) before cooking.
5. Air fry at 375&deg;F for 18-25 minutes. Check for 165&deg;F temperature.

**Cooking Note::
I cook on a wire rack and do not find flippiong necessary. If cooking directly on
pan, flip halfway (at 9-12 minutes).
