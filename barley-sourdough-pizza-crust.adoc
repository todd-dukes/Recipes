= Sourdough Barley Pizza Crust (50/50 Blend)

This recipe converts your previous yeast-based attempt into a long-fermentation sourdough version. The extended cold
ferment (12-24 hours) is critical: it allows the acidity to protect the wheat gluten from barley's enzymes and lets the
beta-glucans fully hydrate, creating a dough sturdy enough to handle without falling apart.

== Ingredients

1 cup active sourdough starter (100% hydration, fed and peaked)
2/3 cup warm water (approx. 5.5 oz)
2 T extra virgin olive oil
1 1/2 C all-purpose flour
1 1/2 C barley flour (Spicy World)
1 1/2 tsp salt
1 tsp sugar (optional, helps browning and initial fermentation) 
== Instructions

Mix: In a large bowl, combine the sourdough starter, warm water, olive oil, and sugar. Whisk until the starter is mostly
     dissolved. Add the all-purpose flour, barley flour, and salt. Stir with a sturdy spoon until a shaggy dough forms
     and no dry flour remains.

Knead: Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface. Knead gently for 2-3 minutes just to ensure even distribution. The
     dough will feel tacky but should not stick aggressively to clean hands. (Alternatively, let it rest for 20 minutes
     after mixing, then perform 3 sets of "stretch and folds" in the bowl instead of kneading).

Cold Ferment: Coat the dough lightly with olive oil, place it in a covered container, and refrigerate for 12 to 24
     hours. (12 hours is minimum for st ructure; 24 hours yields better flavor and digestibility).

Warm Up: Remove the dough from the refrigerator 1 to 2 hours before you plan to shape it. Cold barley dough is stiff and
     prone to tearing; letting it come to room temperature is essential for extensibility.

Divide: Divide the dough into two even portions. Gently shape them into balls and let them rest on the counter, covered,
     for 30 minutes to relax the gluten.

Shape: On a well-floured surface (or directly on parchment paper), gently press and stretch each ball into your desired
     pizza shape. Do not toss or stretch thin like wheat dough; press from the center outward to maintain structure.

Pre-bake: Bake on a pizza stone or cast iron pan in a 400F oven for about 5-7 minutes. This sets the structure before
     toppings are added.

Top: Remove crusts, add sauce, cheese, and toppings.

Final-bake: Return to the oven at 425F-450F until the cheese is browned and the crust is crisp (approx. 8-12 minutes).

== Notes

Starter Adjustment: This recipe assumes your starter is 100% hydration (equal parts flour and water by weight). If your
    starter is stiffer or thinner, adjust water slightly.

Handling: If the dough feels too sticky after warming up, use extra flour on your hands and the surface. Barley dough is
    naturally less elastic than wheat.

Storage: The pre-baked crusts store well in the refrigerator for 3-4 days or can be frozen for up to 3 months.

A1C Benefit: The long fermentation significantly lowers the glycemic impact compared to your previous 1-hour yeast rise,
    making this a much better option for blood sugar management.
