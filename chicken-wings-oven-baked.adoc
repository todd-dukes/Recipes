
= Oven Baked Chicken Wings

.Chicken Rub Ingredients
----
* 1 T flour
* 3 T baking powder
* 2 T Paprika
* 1 T ground black pepper
* 2 T garlic powder
* 2 T onion powder
* 1 T dry mustard
----

.Chicken Rub Directions
----
* 1 batch for 2 people about 1 lb 10 oz chicken wings in package
* put wing pieces in gallon freezer bag with paper towels to dry.
* replace paper towels until they don't get soaked.
* remove paper towels
* 1 T mix with 1 package (about 1 lb 10 oz, 12 pieces) chicken wings
* preheat oven to 250F
* put rack on cookie sheet. Line rack with aluminum foil for easier cleanup.
* place chicken wings not touching on rack
* cook at 250F for 30 minutes
* raise oven temperature to 425F
* cook another 15 to 45 minutes (based on chicken wing size)
----

.Hot Sauce Ingredients
----
* 1/2 C Butter
* 1/2 C Sriracha hot sauce
* 1/2 t granulated garlic
* 1/2 t ground black pepper
----

.Hot Sauce Directions
----
* In saucepan heat ingredients just until butter melts, then keep warm.
* Leftover can be refrigerated and reheated gently in microwave.
* I use 4-6 table spoons sauce per serving (1 batch is two servings)
