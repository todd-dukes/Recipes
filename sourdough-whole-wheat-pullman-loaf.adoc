= Sourdough Pullman Loaf : 50% Whole Wheat
:doctype: article
:notoc:
:nofooter:

== Ingredients

* 250 g white bread flour
* 250 g whole wheat flour
* 350 g lukewarm water
* 100 g active sourdough starter
* 10 g salt

== Instructions

1. Mix the flours and water in a large bowl until no dry bits remain. Let rest (autolyse) for 20 minutes. (Use
   KitchenAid stand mixer with dough hook on speed 2 for about 2 minutes.)

2. Add the starter and salt. Mix thoroughly, then knead or slap-and-fold for about 8 minutes until the dough is smooth
   and stretchy.  (Use KitchenAid stand mixer again on 4 for 8 minutes)

3. Cover the bowl and bulk ferment at room temperature (around 74 F / 23 C) for 8-10 hours, until the dough has roughly
   doubled in size. (Proof in oven. Measured temperature was 80 F)

4. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Gently press into a rectangle the approximate size of your Pullman
   pan base.

5. Fold the dough like a letter (thirds), then roll or press it to fit the length of the pan. Place seam-side down in a
   greased Pullman pan (butter or oil inside works well).

6. Proof in the pan until the dough is about 1/2 to 1 inch below the lid level (1.5-3 hours at room temp, or overnight
   in fridge then 30-60 min warm-up).

7. Preheat oven to 400 F (204 C). Slide the lid fully onto the pan.

8. Bake covered for 30 minutes.

9. Remove or crack the lid, and bake another 15 minutes until golden and internal temp reaches 200 F (93 C).
   (Check temperature and browness. May need up to additional 15 minutes)

10. Remove from pan immediately. Cool completely on a rack before slicing.

== Notes

- Butter inside the pan adds flavor and helps release.
- Aluminum Pullman heats quickly - watch browning.
- For sandwich-style square slices, this shape is ideal.
- Total bake time: ~45-60 minutes depending on oven and pan.
