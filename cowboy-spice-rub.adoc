= Cowboy Spice Rub
:revdate: 2026Sep24
:author: Todd Dukes

== Description
A simple rub for grilled steaks.

== Ingredients
* 2 t ground Coriander
* 2 t dry Mustard
* 2 t ground black pepper
* 1 t Cayenne pepper
* 1/4 cup brown sugar
* 1/4 cup Paprika
* 1/4 cup ground coffee
* 3/4 cup Chili powder

== Preparation
. Combine all ingredients in glass storage bowl and whisk gently with a fork.
. rub both sides of steak generously (maybe 1t - 1T per side)
. Note ingredients are over 1 1/2 cups. Pick big enough storage container.
