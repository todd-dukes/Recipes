= Sourdough Buns (4 buns, tipo 00 flour)

== Ingredients

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Ingredient          | Amount
| Tipo 00 flour       | 300 g
| Water (80F / 27C) | 180-195 g (start with 180 g, adjust for 60-65% hydration)
| Active sourdough starter | 60 g
| Salt                | 6 g
|===

== Equipment

- KitchenAid stand mixer with dough hook
- Digital scale
- Latte thermometer (or similar)
- Proofing oven set to proof mode (80F)
- Bowl
- Parchment-lined baking sheet
- Damp towel or plastic wrap for covering

== Instructions

=== 1. Prepare the Starter

. Remove sourdough starter from refrigerator.
. Scoop out 60 g into a small bowl or container.
. Let it sit at room temperature 30-45 minutes until bubbly and slightly warm (smells tangy but pleasant).

=== 2. Mix the Dough

. In the KitchenAid bowl, combine 180 g water (80F) + 60 g active starter. Stir until mostly dissolved.
. Add 300 g tipo 00 flour + 6 g salt.
. Mix on speed 1 (stir) for ~30 seconds until no dry flour remains.
. Increase to speed 2 and knead 3-4 minutes until smooth and tacky (it should pull away from sides slightly but still stick to bottom).
. If too dry, add water 1 Tbsp at a time; if too sticky, add flour 1 tsp at a time.

=== 3. Autolyse & Stretch-and-Folds

. Cover bowl loosely (or leave uncovered in proofing oven).
. Rest 20 minutes at 80F.
. Perform 3 sets of stretch-and-folds (every 15-20 minutes):
  ** Wet hands to prevent sticking.
  ** Grab one side, stretch gently upward, fold over center.
  ** Rotate bowl 90, repeat 4 folds total per set.
. After final set, dough should feel noticeably stronger and smoother.

=== 4. Bulk Fermentation

. Place bowl in proofing oven (80F, uncovered or loosely covered).
. Ferment until roughly doubled in size, puffy, and bubbly at sides (typically 3-5 hours at 80F; may take longer if starter was cold).
. Check with poke test: gentle indent should spring back slowly (like a marshmallow).
. If slow progress, do one extra stretch-and-fold and continue 30-60 minutes.

=== 5. Divide & Pre-shape

. Turn dough onto unfloured surface.
. Divide into 4 equal pieces (135-140 g each; total dough 545 g : 545 / 4 = 136.25 g per piece).
. Gently round each piece into a loose ball (cup hands and rotate on counter for light tension).

=== 6. Bench Rest

. Cover pieces loosely with damp towel.
. Rest 15-20 minutes (relaxes gluten for easier final shaping).

=== 7. Final Shape & Proof

. Shape each piece into a tight ball: cup hands around dough, drag gently toward you on unfloured counter to create surface tension.
. Place shaped buns seam-side down on parchment-lined baking sheet, spaced apart.
. Cover loosely with damp towel or plastic wrap.
. Proof in proofing oven (80F) 1-2 hours until puffy and poke test shows slow spring-back.

=== 8. Bake

. Preheat oven to 425F (220C) with steam setup if possible (pan of hot water or ice cubes).
. Optional: slash tops lightly or leave plain.
. Bake 18-22 minutes until golden brown and internal temperature reaches 200F.
. For softer crust: wrap in towel after baking for 10 minutes.
. Cool on rack before serving.

== Tips

- Tipo 00 flour gives a very tender, pillowy crumb ideal for soft buns.
- If dough feels stiff during mixing, add water gradually (tipo 00 absorbs more slowly).
- Slow rise? Starter may need refreshing; temperature below 75F slows fermentation significantly.
- Store cooled buns in airtight container up to 2 days or freeze.

Enjoy your soft, homemade sourdough buns!
