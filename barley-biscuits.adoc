= Simple Barley Biscuits

* Yields: 2 biscuits
* Prep time: 10 minutes
* Bake time: 12-15 minutes
* Temperature: 425F (220C)

== Ingredients

* 1 cup barley flour
* 2 tsp baking powder
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 1 T cold butter or lard (ev olive oil for more savory with tender, less flaky texture)
* 3 oz cold milk (or buttermilk)

== Instructions

* Preheat oven to 425F. Line a small baking sheet with parchment paper.
* In a small bowl, whisk together barley flour, baking powder, and salt.
* Cut in the cold fat using a fork or fingers until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
* Add milk gradually, stirring just until a soft dough forms. Do not overmix.
* Turn dough onto a lightly floured surface. Gently pat or roll to 3/4-inch thickness.
* Cut into 2 rounds using a 2.5-inch cutter or glass.
* Place on baking sheet 2 inches apart.
* Bake 12-15 minutes until golden brown.
* Cool on wire rack for 2-3 minutes before serving.

== Notes

* To get flakiness with EVOO, use 4 T EVOO and 2 T water.
