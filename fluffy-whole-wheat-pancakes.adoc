== Fluffy Whole Wheat Pancake Recipe 
:author: tdukes (updated with Grok suggestions)
:revdate: January 2026
:revision: Adds ~50% whole wheat flour for more fiber & nutrients while preserving fluffiness
:icons: font
:icon-set: fas

This is modification of Fluffy Pancake Recipe to more align with inverted food pyramid by replacing half the all-purpose flour
with whole wheat flour.
Yields ~6-9 ultra-fluffy pancakes. The nutty flavor is subtle and the texture stays very light thanks to whipped egg whites and gentle folding.

=== Ingredients

*Wet*

** 6 oz (3/4 cup) milk
** 1 T white vinegar or lemon juice (2 tsp works well too, this should match baking powder amount)
** 2 large eggs - yolks and whites separated (only 1 yolk used)
** 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
** 1/2 tsp vanilla extract (optional)

*Dry*

** 3/8 cup (6 tbsp) all-purpose flour
** 3/8 cup (6 tbsp) whole wheat flour (Can change ratio, but keep total flour 3/4 cup, more whole wheat may require more milk)
** 1 T baking powder
** 1/4 tsp salt
** 1 T powdered sugar

=== Instructions

. *Prepare buttermilk substitute*: Mix 6 oz milk + 1 T vinegar. Let sit at least 5 minutes to curdle.
. *Mix dry ingredients*: Whisk together both flours, baking powder, salt, and powdered sugar in a medium bowl.
. *Prepare wet ingredients*:
  ** Separate eggs (save whites in clean bowl for whipping).
  ** Discard one yolk; lightly whisk the remaining yolk.
  ** Stir melted butter and vanilla into the curdled milk + yolk mixture.
. *Make base batter*: Pour wet mixture into dry. Stir gently until just combined (lumps OK - do NOT overmix).
. *Whip egg whites*: Beat the 2 whites to soft peaks (foamy, holds shape but not stiff). Keep bowl very clean/no grease or trace of egg yolk, or
   the whites may not whip properly.
. *Fold in whites*: Gently fold whipped whites into batter with spatula (10-12 slow folds) to keep it airy.
   ** If batter seems too thick (whole wheat can make it denser), add 1-2 tsp extra milk. If too runny, add 1-2 tsp all-purpose flour.
. *Heat griddle*: Preheat to 350°F. Test with water drop (should sizzle/evaporate). Lightly grease.
. *Cook*: Pour ~1/4 cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form + edges set (~2-3 min). Flip gently; cook 1-2 min more until golden.
. *Serve*: Keep warm in 200°F oven if needed. Top with maple syrup, fruit, etc.

=== Tips for Best Results with Whole Wheat
* Start with the 50/50 flour split - keeps the pancakes tall and tender. If you want even more whole grain later, try 60-75% whole wheat (but expect slightly denser texture).
* White whole wheat flour (if available) gives the lightest result with similar nutrition.
* Whole wheat absorbs more liquid icon:arrow-left[] the extra 1 oz milk helps. Adjust batter consistency before cooking (should be scoopable but not runny).
* Do not overmix after adding whites - preserves air bubbles for fluffiness.
* If first pancake spreads too much or is dense, add 1-2 tsp extra flour to remaining batter.

=== Expected Outcomes
* Texture: Still very fluffy and light (thanks to whipped whites), with a slightly heartier/nuttier bite from whole wheat.
* Flavor: Mild tang + subtle sweetness, plus pleasant whole-grain note.
* Nutrition boost: More fiber, B vitamins, and minerals compared to 100% all-purpose.

Enjoy your healthier super-fluffy pancakes!
