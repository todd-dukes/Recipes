= Oven Slow-Cooked Pork Spare Ribs with BBQ Sauce

== Overview
* **Servings**: 6-8 people (about 8 lbs ribs)
* **Prep Time**: 20-30 minutes
* **Cook Time**: ~6 hours
* **Total Time**: ~6.5 hours
* **Method**: Low and slow oven roast, finished with BBQ sauce

== Ingredients

=== Dry Rub (enough for 8 lbs)
* ¼ cup brown sugar
* 2 Tbsp kosher salt
* 2 Tbsp smoked paprika
* 1 Tbsp garlic powder
* 1 Tbsp onion powder
* 1-2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
* 1-2 tsp chili powder (or cayenne for heat)
* Optional: 1 tsp ground mustard, 1/2 tsp cumin, pinch of cinnamon

=== Other
* 8 lbs pork spare ribs
* Your favorite BBQ sauce (for finishing)
* Aluminum foil

== Instructions

1. **Prepare the Ribs** +
   Remove the thin membrane from the back of the ribs (slide a knife underneath and pull with a paper towel). Pat the
   ribs dry with paper towels.

2. **Apply the Rub** +
   Mix all dry rub ingredients thoroughly. Generously coat both sides of the ribs (about 2-3 Tbsp per side). Let the
   ribs sit at room temperature for 30-60 minutes, or refrigerate uncovered overnight for deeper flavor.

3. **Preheat Oven** +
   Preheat oven to *225F (107C)*.

4. **Cook the Ribs** +
   Place ribs on a foil-lined baking sheet (use a wire rack if available). Cover tightly with another layer of foil. +
   Place in the oven at 12:00 PM and cook for approximately *6 hours*, until internal temperature in the thickest meaty
   part reaches 195-203F, or the ribs pass the bend test (meat pulls back from bones and they bend easily).

5. **Finish with Sauce** +
   Remove ribs from oven. Increase oven temperature to 425-450F (or broil). +
   Brush ribs generously with BBQ sauce on both sides. +
   Return to oven (uncovered) for 5-10 minutes until sauce is caramelized and sticky. Watch closely to avoid burning.

6. **Rest and Serve** +
   Let ribs rest 10 minutes before cutting. Serve hot.

== Tips for Success

* **Wrapping**: Keeping the ribs wrapped in foil for most of the cook creates tender, juicy "fall-off-the-bone" results.
* **Doneness**: Ovens vary. Start checking at the 5-hour mark.
* **Make-Ahead**: Apply rub the night before. Ribs can be held warm in a 170F oven or wrapped in towels in a cooler if finished early.
* **Variations**: For extra bark, unwrap for the last 45-60 minutes before saucing.
