= Hamburger Chili

A simple homemade chili sauce made with ground beef - perfect for chili dogs, loose burgers, fries, or nachos. Makes enough for 8-10 servings.

== Ingredients

* 1 lb lean ground beef (90/10 or leaner to avoid grease)
* 1 small onion, finely diced (or 1-2 Tbsp dried minced onion)
* 1-2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
* 1 (6-8 oz) can tomato sauce (or tomato paste + water to thin)
* 1/2 cup ketchup (gives the classic sweet-tangy taste)
* 1-2 cups water or beef broth (start with 1 cup, add more for consistency)
* 2-3 tsp chili powder (adjust to your preferred heat)
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp salt (or to taste)
* 1/2 tsp black pepper
* 1/2 tsp garlic powder

Optional extras for more flavor:

* 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
* Pinch of cayenne pepper
* 1/4 tsp cinnamon or allspice (for Coney-style depth)
* 1 Tbsp yellow mustard

== Instructions

1. In a skillet or saucepan over medium heat, saute the diced onion until soft (about 4-5 minutes). Add garlic if using and cook 1 more minute.

2. Add the ground beef. Break it up very finely with a spoon or potato masher - tiny crumbles are key for classic chili sauce texture. Cook until fully browned with no pink left. Drain excess fat if necessary.

3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and any optional spices. Cook 1-2 minutes to toast the spices.

4. Add tomato sauce (or paste + water), ketchup, and 1 cup water/broth. Stir well to combine.

5. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low. Simmer uncovered 30-60 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add more liquid if it gets too thick - aim for a spoonable but saucy texture.

6. Taste and adjust seasoning (more chili powder for heat, salt, etc.). For the smoothest texture, mash with a potato masher or use an immersion blender briefly at the end.

== Serving Ideas

Spoon generously over hot dogs in buns. Classic toppings include:

* Yellow mustard
* Diced raw onions
* Shredded cheddar cheese

Also excellent on burgers, fries, or nachos.

This sauce freezes well - portion it out and thaw for quick meals anytime.

Enjoy your Hamburger Chili!
