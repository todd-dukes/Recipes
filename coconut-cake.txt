= Classic Moist Coconut Layer Cake

A fluffy, intensely coconut-flavored layer cake with creamy coconut cream cheese frosting. Perfect for celebrations! Happy Birthday, Lisa!

== Ingredients

=== Cake (two 9-inch layers)
* 2 1/2 cups (285 g) cake flour (or all-purpose flour)
* 2 teaspoons baking powder
* 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 3/4 cup (170 g) unsalted butter, softened
* 1 2/3 cups (333 g) granulated sugar
* 5 large egg whites, room temperature
* 1/2 cup (120 g) sour cream, room temperature
* 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
* 1 teaspoon coconut extract
* 1 cup (240 ml) full-fat canned coconut milk (shake well)
* 1 cup (80 g) sweetened shredded coconut

=== Coconut Cream Cheese Frosting
* 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
* 8 oz (226 g) cream cheese, softened
* 5 cups (600 g) powdered sugar
* 2 tablespoons canned coconut milk
* 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1/2 teaspoon coconut extract
* 1/8 teaspoon salt
* 2 cups (160 g) sweetened shredded coconut (for coating)

== Instructions

. Preheat oven to 350F (177C). Grease two 9-inch round cake pans, line bottoms with parchment paper, then grease again.

. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.

. In a large bowl, beat the butter on medium-high speed until creamy (about 1 minute). Add the granulated sugar and beat until light and fluffy (3 to 4 minutes).

. Add the egg whites one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in the sour cream, vanilla extract, and coconut extract until combined.

. With the mixer on low speed, add the dry ingredients in three additions, alternating with the coconut milk (start and end with dry ingredients). Mix just until combined, do not overmix.

. Gently fold in the 1 cup shredded coconut with a spatula.

. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans. Bake for 22 to 28 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

. Let cakes cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely.

. *Make the frosting*: Beat the butter and cream cheese together until smooth and creamy. Gradually add the powdered sugar, then mix in the coconut milk, vanilla extract, coconut extract, and salt. Beat until light and fluffy.

. *Assemble*: If needed, level the cooled cake layers. Place one layer on a serving plate and spread a generous amount of frosting on top. Add the second layer. Frost the top and sides of the cake.

. Press the remaining 2 cups shredded coconut onto the sides and top of the frosted cake.

. Refrigerate for 30 to 60 minutes before slicing for clean cuts.

== Tips
* Use full-fat canned coconut milk for the best moisture and flavor.
* Room-temperature ingredients help everything blend smoothly.
* The cake stays moist for several days when stored covered in the refrigerator.

Enjoy your beautiful coconut cake!
