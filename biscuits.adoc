= Plain Biscuits (Less Fluffy Version)
Adapted from Ms. Renee's Cooking Class recipe

This version reduces fluffiness by lowering baking powder, using a quick buttermilk substitute (vinegar + milk), and baking at a slightly lower temperature for better control.

== Ingredients

* 1 3/4  cups all-purpose flour
* 2 teaspoons baking powder (reduced from original 1 Tbsp)
*  1/2  teaspoon salt
* 4 Tablespoons chilled butter, cut into small pieces
*  3/4  cup milk + 1 teaspoon white vinegar (or apple cider vinegar / lemon juice)

== Instructions

1. *Preheat oven* to 425F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it.

2. *Prepare the soured milk* (buttermilk substitute): +
   In a measuring cup, add 1 teaspoon vinegar to  3/4  cup milk. +
   Stir gently and let sit for 5-10 minutes until it thickens slightly and looks a bit curdled. +
   (This adds tenderness and helps reduce excessive fluffiness. If dough is too loose later, hold back 1-2 Tbsp.)

3. *Combine dry ingredients*: +
   In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, 2 tsp baking powder, and salt.

4. *Cut in the butter*: +
   Add chilled butter pieces to dry ingredients. +
   Use a pastry blender, two forks, or fingertips to cut/rub butter in until mixture resembles coarse crumbs or fine sand. +
   (Finer texture helps reduce dramatic rise and creates denser biscuits.)

5. *Add the liquid*: +
   Make a well in the center of the dry mixture. +
   Pour in most of the soured milk. +
   Stir gently with a fork just until dough comes together-stir a bit more firmly than usual (about 15-20 stirs) to develop slight structure and reduce fluffiness. +
   Dough should be soft and slightly sticky but hold together. If too dry, add remaining soured milk 1 Tbsp at a time. Avoid overmixing.

6. *Form the biscuits*: +
   Drop spoonfuls (or form loose balls with hands, pressing gently to flatten slightly) onto prepared baking sheet, spacing ~2 inches apart. +
   For even less doming, lightly press tops with fingers or back of spoon to flatten a bit before baking.

7. *Bake*: +
   Bake in preheated 425F oven for 12-15 minutes, or until tops are lightly golden and edges set. +
   (May need 1-2 extra minutes compared to higher-temp recipes-check at 12 minutes.)

8. *Cool & serve*: +
   Let cool on sheet for 1-2 minutes, then transfer to wire rack. Best served warm!

== Yield
About 8-10 biscuits, depending on size.

== Notes
- These biscuits are tender and buttery with a more substantial, less airy texture-ideal for plain eating, gravy, or sandwiches.
- If still too fluffy next time, try reducing baking powder to 1 1/2  tsp or using slightly less milk.
- Experiment and adjust to taste!
